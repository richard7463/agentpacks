01

How a card actually works

A card is not art with a stat block. It is a netlist — a list of NAND gates — minted as a circuit NFT on BNB Chain. The processor evaluates it for free.

STEP 1 · PULL

Draw a rule

Every pack contains exactly one celebrity card and one circuit card. The circuit card is a kind 0 card pointing at one of 12 house netlists.

STEP 2 · TAPE OUT

Make it real

tapeOut(cardId) buys the NAND transistors and mints the circuit NFT into your wallet. The price is the real gate count. Untaped cards cannot be equipped.

STEP 3 · EQUIP

Put it on guard

equip(cardId) binds the circuit to your Agent ID. From then on, any payment can call checkPayment() and get an answer from the gates.

A rule written in a contract can be upgraded. A rule written in a promise can be forgotten. A rule taped into silicon can only be read.

The twelve Kaling

Four families, one job: stand between your money and the dark. The gate count is the weight of the soul.

Trade Corps

They guard the budget: daily cap, single max, growth, count, micro-pass.

Lockdown Keep

They pull the brake: cooldown and full stop the moment fraud is detected.

Security Temple

They ask one question — do you hold an Agent ID? — and high-value half-gate.

Trust Council

They demand agreement: dual sign, quorum, delegation window.
