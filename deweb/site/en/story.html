Chapter One

The Vault at Midnight

III
Chapter Three

The Awakening

Humans have written many rules for AI. Rules in terms get reinterpreted. Rules in code get upgraded. Rules in promises get forgotten.

Then someone did something clumsy and final: etched a rule into NAND gates and taped it on-chain. From then on it cannot be edited, revoked, or bribed.

That night, the programmer on watch saw something strange: the rule had grown a human shape. Stop-loss became a samurai guarding the ledger. Lockdown became a knight dropping the gate. They do not fight for themselves — a rule has no desire. That is exactly why it can be trusted.
